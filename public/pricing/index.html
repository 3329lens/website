Questions

Will the free version lose features?

No. Everything in the open-source scanner today stays in it, under the same licence.

Who handles payment?

Orders will be processed by Polar, our online reseller and Merchant of Record. Polar takes the payment, charges the right sales tax or VAT for your country, and issues the invoice. Card details go to Polar's payment processor and never reach us.

How is a Pro licence checked?

Offline. 3329lens does not send your code, your inventory or any data about your machine anywhere, and a Pro licence will not change that.
